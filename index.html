A deeper experience of Saint Vincent & the Grenadines

Scattered across thirty-two islands and cays, Saint Vincent & the Grenadines rises from volcanic peaks and rainforest into the silky black sand of the mainland's basalt shores, giving way south into the white sand and broken coral shores of the Grenadines, a landscape shaped as much by agriculture, migration and the sea as by geology.

Unlike its more travelled neighbours, Saint Vincent has never built itself around cruise piers or resort chains, leaving its skies darker, its waters quieter and its traditions closer to the surface, a country most travellers have still never set foot on.

SALT is the experiential hospitality house built around that setting, translating its natural and cultural range into original experiences: offshore wildlife expeditions, private stargazing, Vincentian food and wine, craft encounters and aerial exploration.

Each begins with something worth discovering.

The Approach

Curated by place.

SALT looks for what deserves closer attention rather than what is simply passed through: a remarkable natural setting, a specialist with knowledge worth sharing, an ingredient with a story, an unusual perspective on the landscape, or an experience that could only make sense in these islands.

Each is then developed around that discovery, bringing together the right people, operators, equipment and hospitality, so the collection remains intentionally selective rather than expansive.

Private Experiences

One island. An entire archipelago. Your own way through it.

SALT experiences can be enjoyed individually or woven into private days and longer itineraries, beginning with an existing experience or built entirely around a guest's own interests: food, wildlife, culture, astronomy, adventure, or simply the desire to discover something they would not have found alone.

Hospitality Partners: Hotels, Villas, Yachts & Travel Advisors

Destination expertise, beyond the property.

Exceptional hospitality does not end at the hotel door, villa gate or yacht tender.

SALT works with hospitality partners to extend the guest experience across Saint Vincent & the Grenadines, introducing individual experiences, commissioning tailored itineraries or developing programming built specifically for their guests.

Behind each experience, SALT coordinates the appropriate local practitioners, specialist operators and hospitality details, giving partners a single point of contact while preserving the expertise of the people actually delivering it.

How a Partnership Runs

  • 01Select: SALT begins with experiences, people and operators genuinely worth introducing to a guest.
  • 02Compose: The setting, timing, hosting and supporting details are considered as part of one experience rather than added afterwards.
  • 03Coordinate: SALT works between the guest, property and relevant specialist partners so that the complexity remains behind the scenes.
  • 04Host: Where appropriate, SALT provides the context, interpretation and hospitality that turns an activity into an experience of place.

Our Story

Created in Saint Vincent & the Grenadines

SALT began with a question: what becomes possible when Saint Vincent & the Grenadines is explored with the same attention usually reserved for the world's most celebrated destinations? The answer was not to create more tours, but to look more closely, at the people doing exceptional work, the landscapes most visitors never reach, the ingredients and traditions hiding in plain sight, and the natural phenomena that become remarkable once someone knows where and how to look.

SALT brings these elements together as a collection of experiences designed specifically for the islands, curated with the eye of an editor and delivered with the warmth of a host.
