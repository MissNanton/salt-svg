Out here, the sea becomes something else.

Beyond the beaches, anchorages and familiar sailing routes lies a different side of Saint Vincent & the Grenadines. The water deepens. The islands fall behind. And somewhere across the open sea, life is moving. SALT Marine is an offshore wildlife expedition in search of the whales, dolphins, turtles, rays, seabirds and other marine life found in Vincentian waters. Travelling aboard a high-performance powerboat, guests leave the usual island-hopping routes behind for several hours on the open Caribbean Sea. There is no predetermined sighting and no staged encounter. The experience is the search itself.

The Vision

Beyond the surface

Saint Vincent & the Grenadines is usually experienced at the water's edge: beaches, bays, reefs and the brilliant turquoise shallows of the Grenadines. SALT Marine looks further.

Beyond the sheltered coastline, these islands sit within a much larger living environment. Deep water, currents and underwater topography create habitats for marine life that most visitors never go looking for. This is not a leisurely cruise between islands. It is an expedition into that wider landscape — fast, open-air and shaped each day by the sea.

Some days the first sign is a fin breaking the surface. Sometimes it is a gathering of seabirds on the horizon. Sometimes nothing appears at all.

That is the nature of looking for something wild.

The Expedition

Run. Search. Encounter.

Run

Leave the coastline behind

After a short briefing, guests board a high-performance offshore powerboat and head out from the coast, the protected water giving way to deeper blue as the boat moves beyond the familiar sailing routes, salt spray and open horizon marking the run into deeper water.

Search

Read the water

Once offshore, the crew begins reading the water: birds gathering overhead, movement in the distance, an unexpected disturbance in otherwise empty sea. Guests join the search with binoculars, following the conditions rather than a fixed route. There may be long stretches of nothing. And then, suddenly, there may not be.

Encounter

Wild means never guaranteed

When marine life is encountered, the experience slows. Engines ease, distance is respected, and guests watch. Depending on the day and season, encounters may include dolphins, whales, turtles, rays, seabirds and other marine life moving through Vincentian waters — never summoned, fed or pursued, and always on the animal's terms.

For Hospitality Partners

Not another day on the water.

Saint Vincent & the Grenadines already offers exceptional sailing, snorkelling and island-hopping. SALT Marine is designed to offer something different: a private offshore expedition for guests who want adventure rather than another cruise, a fast, purposeful journey beyond the usual routes in search of the wildlife inhabiting the waters surrounding the islands. It is particularly suited to adventurous couples, families with older children, private villa guests and travellers looking for a more active way to experience the destination.

Ways to offer SALT Marine

  • 01The Offshore Expedition: a dedicated half-day search into deeper water, with the route determined by conditions, recent activity and the judgement of the crew.
  • 02The Private Expedition: a privately chartered experience arranged around a villa, yacht or hotel stay, with departure and routing coordinated where operationally possible.
  • 03A SALT Day: SALT Marine as the active chapter of a longer itinerary, returning ashore for Salt & Sorrel, SALT Kitchen or SALT After Dark.

A Note on Wildlife

The sea keeps its own schedule.

SALT Marine takes place in a wild environment. Routes, conditions and sightings change from one expedition to the next. No particular species or encounter can be promised, and the decision to travel always remains subject to weather and sea conditions. We believe this uncertainty is part of what makes the experience worth having. This is not an attraction built around wildlife. It is an opportunity to go looking for it.

Closing Vision

Go further out.

There is more to these waters than the view from shore. We welcome conversations with marine operators, conservation and wildlife specialists, and hospitality partners interested in creating a different kind of experience at sea.
