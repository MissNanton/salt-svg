Start with the guest, not the itinerary.

Some of the best days cannot be selected from a menu.

They begin with a conversation: where you are staying, what you are curious about, what you have already seen, what you do not enjoy and what would make the day feel entirely your own.

SALT Private is the bespoke layer of the house.

It gives guests, concierges and advisors access to SALT's knowledge of the islands without limiting the experience to the existing collection.

Sometimes the answer is one SALT experience. Sometimes several. Sometimes it is something else entirely.

The Vision

Good curation includes knowing what to leave out.

SALT Private is not designed around offering more options.

It exists to make fewer, better decisions.

An itinerary may draw from SALT Marine, Studio, Kitchen, Salt & Sorrel or After Dark. It may introduce a specialist, location or experience not publicly listed.

Or it may deliberately leave half the day empty because nothing would improve it.

The objective is not to fill time. It is to use it well.

How It Begins

Listen. Design. Deliver.

Listen

Understand the guest first.

Every SALT Private arrangement begins with context. The guest. The property. The occasion. The pace of the stay. What they already know about the islands and what they are hoping to find.

Directly or through a concierge, villa manager, yacht team or travel advisor, SALT begins by listening.

Design

Build one coherent experience.

Individual elements are selected because they belong together, not simply because they happen to be available. Timing, transfers, meals, hosts, activity and pauses are considered as part of the same guest journey.

There is no obligation to make the day busier than it needs to be.

Deliver

One experience. One point of contact.

SALT coordinates the moving parts behind the scenes so the guest does not experience a string of unrelated suppliers and bookings.

From the guest's perspective, it should feel simple. That simplicity is part of the work.

Closing Vision

Built around one guest. Not many.

Tell us where you are staying, who you are travelling with and what you would like to understand, taste, see or do differently while you are here. We'll take it from there.
